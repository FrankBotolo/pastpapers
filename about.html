About MalawiPastPapers

Free past papers for every East African student.

Our Mission

MalawiPastPapers.com was created to solve a simple problem: students across East Africa struggle to find past examination papers for study. Many cannot afford paid resources, and papers are scattered across different websites, forums, and WhatsApp groups.

We believe every student deserves free access to quality study materials. Our mission is to build the largest, most organized library of past papers for Malawi, Kenya, and Uganda — completely free, forever.

What We Offer

  • 2,500+ past papers from secondary and university level exams
  • 45+ institutions including Chancellor College, University of Nairobi, Makerere University, and more
  • Study guides written specifically for East African students
  • 100% free — no subscriptions, no paywalls, no registration required

Who We Serve

Our primary audience includes:

  • MSCE, KCSE, and UACE candidates preparing for national exams
  • University students studying for end-of-semester and final exams
  • Teachers and lecturers looking for reference materials
  • Anyone preparing for examinations in Malawi, Kenya, or Uganda

How We're Funded

MalawiPastPapers.com is supported by Google AdSense advertising. This allows us to keep all papers free while covering hosting and maintenance costs. We are committed to maintaining a clean, non-intrusive ad experience that doesn't interfere with studying.

Contribute

Have past papers to share? We welcome submissions from students, teachers, and alumni. Every paper you submit helps thousands of fellow students. Submit a paper today.
